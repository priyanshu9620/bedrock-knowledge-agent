# Glossary

- CL — casual leave. SL — sick leave. EL — earned leave.
- CTC — cost to company, the total annual compensation.
- L-grades — engineering levels L1 (junior) to L7 (principal). L5 is senior staff.
- POSH — Prevention of Sexual Harassment; the IC is the Internal Committee under POSH.
- MDM — mobile device management.
- IC (in incidents) — incident commander, the single owner of an S1/S2 incident.
- Travel desk — the team that books flights and hotels, reachable at travel@meridianlabs.example.
