# IT Security Policy

- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on all company accounts.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and must be rotated every 90 days.
- Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled; this is enforced by the device management profile.
- USB mass storage is disabled on company laptops.
- Access to production systems requires the company VPN and a ticket reference.
- Suspected phishing emails must be reported to security@meridianlabs.example using the "Report phishing" button.
- Any suspected security incident must be reported within 1 hour of discovery to the security on-call.
- Sharing credentials, including API keys, over chat or email is prohibited.
