# Benefits

- Health insurance: ₹5,00,000 family floater covering the employee, spouse and up to 2 children. An optional
  top-up of ₹5,00,000 and optional parental cover can be bought at the employee's cost.
- Term life insurance: 3 times annual CTC.
- Accident insurance: ₹25,00,000.
- Provident fund: 12% employee and 12% employer contribution on basic salary.
- Gratuity: payable after 5 years of continuous service.
- Wellness allowance: ₹10,000 per year for gym, sports or therapy.
- Learning budget: ₹25,000 per year for courses, books and conferences, approved by the manager.
- Employee referral bonus: ₹50,000 for engineering roles, paid after the referred employee completes 3 months.
