# Code of Conduct

- Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. Complaints under POSH go to the Internal Committee (IC) at
  posh-ic@meridianlabs.example and are acknowledged within 7 days.
- Gifts from vendors or customers above ₹2,000 in value must be declared to the manager and may have to be returned.
- Conflicts of interest, including outside employment, must be declared in writing.
- The whistleblower channel is anonymous and reaches the audit committee directly.
- Confidential information must not be shared outside the company without a signed NDA.
