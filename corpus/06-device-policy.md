# Device Policy

- Company laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
- A lost or stolen device must be reported to IT within 24 hours so it can be remotely wiped.
- If a device is lost through negligence, up to 50% of the replacement cost may be recovered from the employee.
- Personal phones may be used for work email only if enrolled in mobile device management (MDM).
- Devices must be returned within 3 working days of the last working day.
