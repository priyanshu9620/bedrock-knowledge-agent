# Leave Policy

## Entitlements (per calendar year)
- Casual leave (CL): 12 days per year, credited in full on 1 January. Casual leave does not carry forward.
- Sick leave (SL): 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days.
- Earned leave (EL): 18 days per year, accrued at 1.5 days per month. Unused earned leave carries forward up to a
  maximum balance of 30 days. Earned leave above 30 days lapses on 31 December.
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave.
- Paternity leave: 2 weeks of paid leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth.
- Bereavement leave: 5 days.

## Applying
- Planned leave must be applied at least 5 working days in advance in the HR portal.
- Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days needs approval from the skip-level manager.
- Earned leave is encashed at exit at the basic salary rate; casual and sick leave are not encashed.
- Employees on probation can take casual leave and sick leave but cannot take earned leave until probation ends.
