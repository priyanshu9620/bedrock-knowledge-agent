# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

- Hybrid schedule: 3 days a week in the office, Tuesday to Thursday. Monday and Friday are remote by default.
- Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST; everyone is expected to be reachable in these hours.
- Working from another city is allowed for up to 4 weeks per year with manager approval.
- Working from outside India needs approval from people-ops at least 30 days in advance.
- A one-time home-office stipend of ₹15,000 is available to set up a desk, chair or monitor.
