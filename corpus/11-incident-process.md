# Production Incident Process

- Severity levels: S1 (customer-facing outage), S2 (major degradation), S3 (minor issue), S4 (cosmetic).
- S1 incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and have an incident commander assigned.
- S2 incidents must be acknowledged within 30 minutes.
- On-call rotations are weekly and swap on Monday at 10:00 IST.
- Every S1 and S2 incident gets a blameless postmortem within 5 working days.
- Customer communication for S1 goes out within 60 minutes through the status page.
- A helpdesk ticket must be filed for any incident so that it is tracked.
