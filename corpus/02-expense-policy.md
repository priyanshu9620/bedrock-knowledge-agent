# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Limits
- Meals while travelling: up to ₹800 per day for domestic travel and ₹2,500 per day for international travel.
- Local cab or auto: actuals; a receipt is required for any single ride above ₹300.
- Home internet: up to ₹1,000 per month, reimbursed quarterly.
- Mobile phone bill: up to ₹750 per month.
- Team outing: up to ₹1,500 per person per quarter, approved by the team lead.

## Rules
- Receipts are mandatory for any expense above ₹500. Below ₹500 a self-declaration is accepted.
- Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date; older claims are rejected.
- Claims are approved by the reporting manager and paid with the next payroll cycle.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable.
- Claims above the category limit are reimbursed only up to the limit; the excess is borne by the employee.
