# Travel Policy

- Flights: economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or
  longer, only for employees at grade L5 and above.
- Hotels: up to ₹6,000 per night in metro cities (Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) and
  up to ₹4,000 per night elsewhere.
- Bookings must be made through the travel desk at least 14 days before departure, except for customer emergencies.
- Travel insurance is mandatory for all international travel and is arranged by the travel desk.
- Per diem for international travel is USD 50 per day when meals are not provided by the host.
- Personal travel combined with business travel must be declared; the company pays only the business portion.
